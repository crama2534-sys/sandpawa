SandPawa

Renewable thermal battery that stores solar heat during the day and delivers clean heat at night.

Contact Us Learn More

The Problem

Solar energy is abundant during the day, yet heat is still needed after sunset. Most households and small farms continue to rely on firewood, charcoal, electricity or LPG for nighttime heating and poultry brooding.

The missing piece is affordable, long-duration thermal energy storage.

The Solution

SandPawa is a low-cost renewable thermal battery designed for real-world conditions.

It captures solar heat during the day, stores it in an insulated sand-based system, and releases controlled heat at night — without continuous fuel or electricity use.

  • ✓ Stores heat for 8–12 hours
  • ✓ Uses locally available materials
  • ✓ No continuous fuel required
  • ✓ Designed for local manufacturing
  • ✓ Clean and reliable

Capture renewable heat when it is available.
Store it.
Deliver it when it is needed.

Applications

Nighttime Space Heating

Provide clean, affordable warmth for homes after sunset without continuous electricity or fuel combustion.

Poultry Brooding

Deliver reliable, controlled heat for chicks during the critical early period — even at night.
